Resolve one exact current NYC non-condominium tax lot from official DCP PLUTO. Supply parcel_id=ten-digit BBL, jurisdiction=US-NY-NYC,country=US; alternatively platform=nyc_pluto,native_id=BBL,country=US. No address/geocoder, condo billing/unit-lot or historical split/merge inference. Success costs 50 credits and stores account-owned property/record/assessment observations24h. Confidence unknown, method exact_current_nyc_tax_lot_bbl. Source: NYC Open Data DCP PLUTO. Independent wrapper, no title/ownership/unit/availability verification.

<Note>Check [live capabilities](https://realtycrawl.dev/v1/meta/capabilities?platform=nyc_pluto). Independent wrapper for NYC Open Data. Existing idempotency replay remains a prior snapshot and can outlive 24h selector retention.</Note>

First observe an Austin single-family Zillow sale listing in this account. Resolve with platform=zillow, native_id=its ZPID and country=US. A fresh pinned source detail independently confirms the ZPID and canonical URL, costing 50 credits on success. The returned property has a zillow_zpid reference, separate from NYC BBL identity.

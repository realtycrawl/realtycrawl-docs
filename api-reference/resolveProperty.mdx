openapi: "POST /v1/properties/resolve"

Resolve one exact current NYC non-condominium tax lot from official DCP PLUTO. Supply parcel_id=ten-digit BBL, jurisdiction=US-NY-NYC,country=US; alternatively platform=nyc_pluto,native_id=BBL,country=US. No address/geocoder, condo billing/unit-lot or historical split/merge inference. Success costs 50 credits and stores account-owned property/record/assessment observations24h. Confidence unknown, method exact_current_nyc_tax_lot_bbl. Source: NYC Open Data DCP PLUTO. Independent wrapper, no title/ownership/unit/availability verification.

<Note>Check [live capabilities](https://realtycrawl.dev/v1/meta/capabilities?platform=nyc_pluto). Independent wrapper for NYC Open Data. Existing idempotency replay remains a prior snapshot and can outlive 24h selector retention.</Note>

## Zillow source-scoped alternative

Resolve an Austin single-family Zillow sale home directly with platform=zillow, native_id=its ZPID and country=US. No prior listing lookup is required. A fresh pinned source detail independently confirms the ZPID and canonical URL, costing 50 credits on success. The returned property has a zillow_zpid reference, separate from NYC BBL identity. Retained property details and one explicitly associated advertisement cost 1 credit each and last 24 hours. Errors and replay cost 0. No cadastral/cross-portal match, title, ownership, private AVM or complete listing inventory is inferred.

The distinct pinned native-property provider returns the exact ZPID, bare canonical URL, market, home type, sale status and USD asking price. Source update time is unknown; observed_at is retrieval time. Unitless areas, contacts, precise location, narrative, photos and AVM fields are omitted. This property mode does not produce listing history.

## US address composite alternative

Resolve a US street address directly with `address={formatted:...,country:"US"}` or `address={line1,city,region or postal_code,country:"US"}`. The composite queries up to three pinned Apify sources — county assessor records (published owner of record, tax-roll assessed/market values, parcel characteristics, sale and tax history), recorder deed/mortgage/lien index documents, and a Zillow address detail page (portal specs, photos, Zestimate and rent estimate). Success costs 50 credits and stores one combined account-owned observation for 24 hours; retained property, record, assessment, transaction, valuation and listing reads then cost 1 credit each.

Owner fields reflect the published assessor owner of record, not verified title. Recorded documents are recorder-index rows: mortgage, lien and lender names are filed-instrument evidence, not a lien search or current debt status. Assessor and portal values are tax-roll or model estimates, not appraisals or AVMs. The assessor source requires a provider minimum above the standard per-run allowance; it reports an explicit unavailable outcome rather than exceeding the cap, so assessor fields may be absent. County route coverage varies; uncovered jurisdictions return an explicit coverage outcome. This binding is documentation-backed beta, not fresh live source verification.

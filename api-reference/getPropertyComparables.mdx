---
title: "Read an unadjusted provider-selected comparable home"
openapi: "GET /v1/properties/{property_id}/comparables"
---

Use this account’s unexpired Austin single-family Zillow property from source-scoped resolution. One fresh provider-selected comparable-home observation, limit 1 (default 1), no cursor. Omit basis or use source_recommendation; closed_sale, active_listing, rental_listing and closed_lease bases are unsupported. Success costs 50 credits; no data, errors and prior-response replay cost zero.

The provider’s dedicated comparable_homes field establishes the selection relationship. Returned positive-price records have price.kind=unknown: the source does not establish asking, sold or estimated-value meaning. Zero-price sold rows are omitted. Event date, distance, adjustments and listing remain null. This is an unadjusted source recommendation, without verified closed sales, arm’s-length consideration, exhaustive matching, appraisal or valuation guarantees. Exact address/geometry, media, contacts and owner information are omitted. Comparison property IDs are source-scoped and are not automatically retained selectors for getProperty.

<Note>Check [live capabilities](https://realtycrawl.dev/v1/meta/capabilities?platform=zillow). The original property selector is private to this account for 24 hours. Existing seven-day idempotency replay returns a prior snapshot and can outlive selector retention.</Note>

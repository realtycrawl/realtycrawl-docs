---
title: "Pagination"
---

Use only continuation cursors returned by the API, and keep each page within your approved request budget.

## Stored collection cursors

Media and history collection cursors are signed and bound to your account, resource, observation and page size. Send next_cursor as cursor and preserve the original limit. They stop working when the observation expires after 24 hours.

```bash
curl "https://realtycrawl.dev/v1/listings/$LISTING_ID/media?limit=1" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $REALTYCRAWL_API_KEY" \
  -H "X-Max-Credits: 1"
```

## Search windows

Search adapters return bounded windows and may have no continuation cursor. Increasing limit is a new request, not a continuation guarantee. A source can return fewer results than requested or more than the provider’s requested limit; RealtyCrawl enforces its output bound.

## Avoid automatic collection loops

Inspect the current response and budget before fetching another page. A source’s lack of pagination does not prove inventory completeness. Preserve source-order semantics and completeness warnings.

## Catalog and auction markets

All of these selectors use location.kind=source_region, location.platform matching the platform ID and the stated country. Sale: kleinanzeigen (DE, bremen); commercialrealestate_au (AU, australia); properati (AR, argentina); infocasas (UY, montevideo); estately (US, los-angeles-ca); opensooq (JO, jordan); midland_hk (HK, hong-kong); bayut_sa (SA, riyadh); toctoc (CL, santiago); adondevivir (PE, peru); plusvalia (EC, quito). Rental: vivanuncios (MX, aguascalientes). These are bounded, verified source windows, not nationwide coverage guarantees.

Adondevivir and Plusvalia development advertisements are labeled in extensions.observation_kind. They do not represent individual units: price.amount, bedrooms and unit areas remain unknown. Toctoc UF ranges preserve unit_code=UF without pretending UF is a currency or substituting a CLP conversion. Commercial tender prices remain unknown. Vivanuncios rent period is unknown where the source does not specify it.

Auction sources: auction (US, chicago-il), xome (US, tx) and hubzu (US, tx). Platform or unified listing search requires transaction=auction. Dedicated POST /v1/auctions/search takes one matching platform in platforms and the documented source_region, with limit. It costs 50 credits per successful source and does not accept query or cursor. Hubzu currently covers the sampled page-2 Texas window.

GET /v1/auctions/\{auction_id\} returns the requesting account’s stored auction observation for 1 credit, retained for 24 hours. Starting bids, current bids, estimated values and reserve disclosures remain distinct. Unknown bids are null. Event dates and statuses are source observations, not guarantees that bidding is open; the API does not submit bids.

Additional excluded samples: HouseSigma, LoopNet, Crexi and Aqarmap failed upstream; Urbania and Movoto returned no listings. Bproperty lacks verified canonical listing identity; RealtyTrac returned a conflicting market/URL and estimated value rather than a verified asking price. These sources remain unavailable.

## Stay observations and Austria

Airbnb: airbnb, GB, source_region=london, currency GBP. Vrbo: vrbo, US, source_region=orlando-fl, currency USD. Use POST /v1/stays/search with one matching platform, location, check_in, check_out, guests=\{"adults":2\}, currency and limit=1–50. Dates must describe a future stay of 1–30 nights. Children, infants, pets, locale, accommodation filters and cursors are not currently supported.

Stay search costs 50 credits. GET /v1/stays/\{stay_id\} costs 1 credit and returns this account’s stored observation for 24 hours. Airbnb totals and Vrbo nightly observations remain separate in extensions.source_price. Taxes, fees and availability remain unknown. These are not booking quotes, full calendars, reviews or guarantees that a property can be reserved.

ImmoScout24 Austria: immoscout24_at, AT; source_region=wien with transaction=sale. The verified Vienna apartment feed excludes development-project aggregates. Source room counts stay in property.rooms rather than being treated as bedrooms.

Vivanuncios location hierarchies may start with a neighborhood or a city. The adapter uses the explicit CIUDAD, PROVINCIA and PAIS labels, preserving city/region and withholding precise location.

Agoda returned named-hotel metadata but no result for the tested city-search contract, so it remains unavailable. A LoopNet auction candidate returned no results; Point2Homes denied the bounded API request. These do not enable those platforms.

## Shared operations

The 98 listing adapters support platform search, unified search, stored listing retrieval and stored media. Airbnb and Vrbo use the separate stay search and stored-stay endpoints. Zillow supports canonical URL resolution and source-reported price history. Rightmove supports fresh native-ID lookup and canonical URL resolution. OneRoof supports stored open-home schedules in UTC. Stored observations belong to the requesting account and expire after 24 hours.

Source records retain their original currencies and area units. Hidden search prices, auction text, price ranges and ambiguous room counts are not converted into exact asking prices or bedroom counts.

## Understand completeness

Search results are bounded source observations, not complete inventories. Source order is preserved. A missing continuation cursor does not mean that every listing was collected. Source fees, run limits and provider availability can constrain results. Preserve warnings, source outcomes and completeness metadata.

## Shared operations

The 140 listing adapters support platform search, unified search, stored listing retrieval and stored media. Airbnb, Vrbo, Agoda, Trip.com and Booking.com use the separate stay search and stored-stay endpoints. Zillow supports canonical URL resolution and source-reported price history. Rightmove supports fresh native-ID lookup and canonical URL resolution. OneRoof supports stored open-home schedules in UTC. Stored observations belong to the requesting account and expire after 24 hours.

Source records retain their original currencies and area units. Hidden search prices, auction text, price ranges and ambiguous room counts are not converted into exact asking prices or bedroom counts.

## Understand completeness

Search results are bounded source observations, not complete inventories. Source order is preserved. A missing continuation cursor does not mean that every listing was collected. Source fees, run limits and provider availability can constrain results. Preserve warnings, source outcomes and completeness metadata.

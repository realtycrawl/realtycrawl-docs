These twenty additions expose locality only, with no exact street address or coordinates. Empty media is a valid source outcome. They share the existing sale-search and stored-resource operations; they do not enable rental, valuation, professional, or development endpoints.

For every source_region selector, set location.platform to the platform slug and location.country to the country above.

Every location selector above includes location.country. All adapters require transaction=sale. Start with limit=1. Only capability-advertised filters are accepted; source_region paths are not free-text geocoding.

## Shared operations

All 60 adapters support platform search, unified search, stored listing retrieval and stored media. Zillow supports canonical URL resolution and source-reported price history. Rightmove supports fresh native-ID lookup and canonical URL resolution. OneRoof supports stored open-home schedules in UTC. Stored observations belong to the requesting account and expire after 24 hours.

Source records retain their original currencies and area units. Hidden search prices, auction text, price ranges and ambiguous room counts are not converted into exact asking prices or bedroom counts.

## Unavailable coverage

The remaining 140 sources and 32 operation definitions are not enabled. Rentals, merged property records, valuations, dedicated agent/agency APIs, auction-event APIs, buildings, stays and async jobs require separate implementation and verification. Licensed MLS and public-record feeds need their own access agreements. BuyRentKenya and iProperty failed the latest capped tests; Casa.it returned no results. Other failed samples include Flatmates and Dubizzle Egypt.

Current excluded samples: Imobiliare.ro and Compass exceeded the synchronous deadline; Hemnet and Zolo returned no usable records. ImmoScout24 was interrupted by a client connection failure; Imovirtual did not complete successfully. Sahibinden returned an upstream error and MagicBricks returned no listings. These failures do not enable those sources.

Additional deferred samples: Beike returned an error record; LIFULL HOME’S and Century 21 returned no listings; PortalInmobiliario, ImmoScout24 Switzerland and Zonaprop failed. Inmuebles24 and 591 need development-specific normalization; Avito lacks an unambiguous sale marker; QuintoAndar returned unpublished records without canonical listing URLs. Bikroy source identity needs further validation. These remain unavailable.

## Understand completeness

Search results are bounded source observations, not complete inventories. Source order is preserved. A missing continuation cursor does not mean that every listing was collected. Source fees, run limits and provider availability can constrain results. Preserve warnings, source outcomes and completeness metadata.
